title: "Barcelona Big Blues Band"
---

Blues, swing and rhythm and blues in big band format from Barcelona.

Led by double bassist Ivan Kovacevic, the Barcelona Big Blues Band is one of the most powerful and in-demand ensembles on the country's jazz, swing and rhythm and blues circuit. A sonic torrent in the tradition of the great bands, such as T-Bone Walker's or Johnny Otis's, they have backed first-rate soloists like Ray Gelato, Mike Sanchez, Drew Davies or Dani Nel·lo.
