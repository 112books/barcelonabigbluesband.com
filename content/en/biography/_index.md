title: "Biography"
description: "The Barcelona Big Blues Band, led by double bassist Ivan Kovacevic."
---

Traditionally, Barcelona has produced good jazz and swing Big Bands.

From the early seventies to the present day, many of the city's musicians and arrangers have been seduced by the power and versatility characteristic of this kind of ensemble. But until now, none of them had done so with the intention of dedicating themselves to blues and rhythm and blues. The Barcelona Big Blues Band, under the direction of double bassist Ivan Kovacevic, brings together a group of fine musicians who create a sound that is energetic, wild and at the same time sophisticated. Through his arrangements, Kovacevic applies to the Big Band all the experience and taste he has built up in small combos. The result is a sonic torrent in the tradition of bands such as T-Bone Walker's or Johnny Otis's.

Focused on accompanying the best national and international rhythm'n'blues artists — which gives them a broad and ever-changing sound from one concert to the next — they have collaborated with Dani Nel·lo, Ray Gelato, Myriam Swanson, Mike Sanchez, Jonathan Herrero, Drew Davies, Greg Piccolo, Steve Lucky & Carmen Gettit, Mark Tortorici, Agustí Burriel, Blas Picón, Big Dani Pérez, Lluís Coloma, Santos Puertas, etc., which has taken them to jazz and blues festivals across Catalonia and to landmark venues such as the Jamboree and the Palau de la Música.

They released their first CD, "Sax attack", with Dani Nel·lo (El Toro Records, August 2014), followed by a series of EPs with Mike Sanchez, Myriam Swanson, Agustí Burriel and Drew Davies.

From one concert to the next, the Barcelona Big Blues Band achieves its only goal: making the audience enjoy it the same way they do… With all their soul!
