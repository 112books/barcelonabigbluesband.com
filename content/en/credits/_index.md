This website collects photographs, chronicles and graphic material from the Barcelona Big Blues Band. Below is the authorship of each item.

## Photography

### Chronicles and albums

All the chronicles and photo albums in the [Photos](../photos/) section are by **Joan Linux** ([blog.pocallum.cat](https://blog.pocallum.cat/), [pocallum.cat](https://pocallum.cat/)).

### Other images

All photographs on this site, except for album covers and the main hero image on the home page, are by **Joan Linux**.

## Discography

Album covers come from the original materials of each release. The graphic design and illustrators of the covers are **to be confirmed**. This page will be updated once known.
