## What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser when you visit a website, allowing information about your browsing to be remembered. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) also exist and are collectively referred to as "cookies".

## What cookies do we use?

**This website does not use its own tracking or advertising cookies.** We do not need your consent for cookies because we do not set any for tracking purposes.

## Cookie-free analytics

Visit statistics are measured with **GoatCounter**, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that **does not use cookies** and does not collect identifying personal data. We do not track you across websites or profile you.

## Third-party services

Some embedded content or links may load external services that, if you interact with them, may set their own cookies:

- **YouTube videos:** we use the `youtube-nocookie.com` domain, which does not set tracking cookies until the video is played.
- **Links to social networks and platforms** (Instagram, Facebook, Spotify, etc.): these only open when you click them, at which point those services' cookie policies apply.

## How you can manage cookies

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Here are the help pages of the most common browsers:

- Mozilla Firefox

- Microsoft Edge

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if we change the services we use. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
