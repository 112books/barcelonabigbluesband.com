Planning an event and want the music to sound like a film? The **Barcelona Big Blues Band**
brings a rhythm'n'blues big band to your stage: from a four-piece combo up to
**13 musicians with a nine-piece horn section**, playing blues, swing and jazz.

## Which events

- **Corporate**: presentations, gala dinners, end-of-year parties, openings.
- **Weddings and private celebrations**: drinks, dinner and dancing.
- **Local festivals and public events**: squares, large stages, community celebrations.
- **Venues and concert series**: ticketed shows and regular programming.

## What is included

- A line-up sized to the room and the budget (4-13 musicians).
- Technical rider sent well ahead so the sound is settled.
- A rhythm'n'blues, swing and jazz repertoire, with requests by arrangement.
- A fixed written quote and a reply within 24 hours.

## Why us

- **More than 15 years** in residence at Barcelona's Jamboree Jazz Club.
- **13 albums** with guests such as Dani Nel·lo, Ray Gelato, Mike Sánchez and Myriam Swanson.
- Stages such as the [Palau de la Música Catalana](https://www.palaumusica.cat/) and festivals such as [Mas i Mas](https://masimas.com/) or [San Javier](https://jazz.sanjavier.es/) (Murcia).
- A stable band: every gig is played by the same musicians you see on this website.

## How it works

1. You write to us with **date, venue, type of event and capacity**.
2. We propose a **line-up** and send the **technical rider**.
3. We agree a **quote** and hold the date.
4. We coordinate with the sound engineer and soundcheck on the day.

Start here: [request a quote](/en/booking/). If you want to hear it first, watch the
[videos](/en/videos/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you book a blues band for a wedding?

Yes. The usual set-up is the 4-7 piece combo for drinks and dinner and the full big band for the
party; both can be combined on the same day.

### Which line-up works best for a corporate event?

A 4-7 piece combo at controlled volume for presentations and receptions; a 13-piece big band (usually with one or two guest soloists) for
for gala dinners and end-of-year parties.

### How far in advance should you book the band?

Four to eight weeks for corporate and private events. For festivals, often months: it is always
worth asking about availability.

### What does the quote include?

Line-up and number of musicians, performance length, travel, sound equipment per the technical
rider and soundcheck. Fixed and in writing.
