The **Barcelona Big Blues Band** is Europe's largest rhythm & blues big band, led by double bassist Ivan Kovačević. Resident at Barcelona's Jamboree for more than a decade, they bring their repertoire of rhythm 'n' blues, swing and big band music to venues, festivals and concert series across the continent.

Flexible line-up: from a combo up to a 15-piece orchestra with a seven-piece horn section, always joined by top-tier special guests.
