The **Barcelona Big Blues Band** is Europe's largest rhythm & blues big band, led by double bassist [Ivan Kovacevic](https://ivankovacevic.com/). Resident at Barcelona's [Jamboree Jazz Club](https://www.jamboreejazz.com/) for more than a decade, they bring their repertoire of rhythm 'n' blues, swing and big band music to venues, festivals and concert series across the continent.

Flexible line-up: from a combo up to a 15-piece orchestra with a seven-piece horn section, always joined by top-tier special guests.
