Barcelona is one of Europe's live blues capitals. It is not a city of large halls devoted to the
genre, but of **small and mid-sized venues where blues is played every week**, with a loyal
audience and musicians who ply their trade there. This guide covers where to hear it, which
festivals matter and how to book a blues band for a concert or an event.

## Where to hear live blues in Barcelona

- **[Jamboree](https://www.jamboreejazz.com/)** (plaça Reial): the reference room, with jazz and
  blues almost every night and the Barcelona Big Blues Band's residency for more than a decade.
- **La Paloma**: historic Raval venue, with soul, funk and blues nights and the Sax-o-Rama festival.
- **Luz de Gas**: bigger shows and tribute nights, with a broad audience.
- **Paral·lel 62**: multi-purpose space with series devoted to Black American music.
- **Neighbourhood rooms and jams**: the best way to discover players is to go to the weekly
  *jam sessions*; the same musicians often turn up later on the big stages.

If you want a full-band blues night, check the
[Barcelona Big Blues Band calendar](/en/agenda/): every concert is listed with date, venue and tickets.

## Blues festivals in Catalonia and beyond

- **Festival Internacional de Blues de Cerdanyola**: the oldest and most established in the country.
- **Festival de Blues de Barcelona**: concerts spread across the city.
- **Vijazz** (Vilafranca del Penedès): jazz and blues in a small town, in summer.
- **Terrassa Jazz Festival**: a broad programme with dedicated blues nights.
- **Cambrils International Music Festival**: stages by the sea.
- Beyond Catalonia, the **San Javier Jazz Festival** (Murcia) and the **Pertuis Big Band Festival**
  (France) are two events the Barcelona Big Blues Band has played.

## The Barcelona Big Blues Band

The Barcelona outfit —led by double bassist [Ivan Kovacevic](https://ivankovacevic.com/)— is
Europe's largest rhythm'n'blues big band: up to **15 musicians with a seven-piece horn section**,
with a configurable line-up from combo upwards. Its sound comes out of the T-Bone Walker and
Johnny Otis band tradition and it has backed artists such as Dani Nel·lo, Myriam Swanson,
Ray Gelato, Mike Sánchez, Lluís Coloma, Wax & Boogie, Drew Davies and Spencer Evoy.

## How to book a blues band in Barcelona

1. **Date and place**: give the date, the space (indoor or outdoor) and the expected capacity.
2. **Line-up**: a combo (4-7 musicians) for smaller rooms; a big band (up to 15) for festivals,
   large halls and corporate events.
3. **Technical rider**: the band sends its sound and stage requirements; the rider lives on the
   [booking page](/en/booking/).
4. **Quote**: fixed according to line-up, travel and duration. Reply within 24 hours.
5. **Lead time**: for corporate and private events, 4-8 weeks is comfortable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can you hear live blues in Barcelona?

The key venues are Jamboree (plaça Reial), La Paloma, Luz de Gas and Paral·lel 62. Weekly jam
sessions and monthly blues and rhythm'n'blues series run alongside the regular programmes.

### What is the best blues festival in Catalonia?

The Cerdanyola and Barcelona blues festivals are the most established, alongside Vijazz in
Vilafranca, the Terrassa Jazz Festival, Cambrils and, beyond Catalonia, San Javier and Pertuis.

### How do you book a blues band for an event?

Give the date, venue, type of event and expected audience. The band proposes a line-up, sends the
technical rider and a fixed quote. We recommend 4-8 weeks' notice.

### What is the difference between a combo and a big band?

A combo has 4-7 musicians and a more intimate sound. A big band adds seven horns and reaches
15 musicians: more power, a wider repertoire and much bigger stage presence.

### Does the Barcelona Big Blues Band play outside Barcelona?

Yes: Palau de la Música Catalana, Teatro Juan Bravo (Segovia), Auditorio elCírculo (Logroño),
l'Auditori Pau Casals (el Vendrell), Vinseum (Vilafranca) and festivals such as San Javier or Pertuis.
