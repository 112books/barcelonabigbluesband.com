The **Barcelona Big Blues Band** is Europe's largest rhythm'n'blues big band: up to
**13 musicians with a nine-piece horn section**, led by double bassist
[Ivan Kovacevic](https://ivankovacevic.com/). It holds a residency at Barcelona's
[Jamboree Jazz Club](https://www.jamboreejazz.com/) and has done for more than 15 years.

## Why a big band and not a combo

A big band is not "a bigger band": it is a different language. With written arrangements for
seven horns the sound gains body, dynamics and an ending a combo simply cannot deliver. For a
festival, a large hall, a local festival or a corporate event, the visual and sonic impact is on
another level.

## Line-ups

- **Combo** · 4-7 musicians · small rooms, cocktails and receptions.
- **Intermediate** · 8-11 musicians · mid-sized rooms, terraces and private parties.
- **Full big band** · 13 musicians · festivals, theatres, squares and corporate events.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a big band?

An ensemble with a full horn section — seven pieces: saxes, trumpets and trombone — plus a rhythm
section of piano, double bass, guitar and drums, playing written arrangements with far more power
than a combo.

### How many musicians are in the Barcelona Big Blues Band?

13 musicians with a nine-piece horn section and rhythm section in the full line-up, usually joined by one or two guest soloists (up to 15 on stage); it also scales down to a combo or
intermediate formats.

### Where does the big band play in Barcelona?

Residency at [Jamboree](https://jamboreejazz.com/) (plaça Reial); also [Palau de la Música](https://www.palaumusica.cat/),
[La Paloma](https://www.barcelona.cat/culturapopular/es/detall/sala-de-festes-la-paloma_99400504492.html), [Luz de Gas](https://luzdegas.com/), [Paral·lel 62](https://paral-lel62.cat/) and festivals such as
[Mas i Mas](https://masimas.com/), Grec and Sax-o-Rama.

24 hours.
