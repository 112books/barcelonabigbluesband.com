Pianist and singer born in Montevideo (Uruguay) and based in Barcelona, known for his New Orleans sound, he represents a generation devoted to searching for the roots of Afro-American music.

**Fact file:** Piano and vocals · Montevideo, 1992 · El Club del Jump, Alberto Burguez Trio, The Flying Roots. Brother of guitarist Martín Burguez; he moved to Barcelona in 2022.
