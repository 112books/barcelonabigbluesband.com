The artistic couple who in the nineties were part of the phenomenon known as the Swing Revival in the USA continue to influence generations from their base in San Francisco.

## Rock·a·Bell Christmas Party

It started as a simple Christmas gig in 2014. Since then, every year it turns carols into works of swing and blues and venues into an authentic express bonfire. Once again, the Barcelona Big Blues Band proves that with their own rock'n'roll arrangements, the right guests and sweat on stage, they can transport the audience to another era and fill the dance floor.

## Discography

Over the years, the Barcelona Big Blues Band has captured its characteristic sound and put its energy and elegance into several recordings, adapting to the essence of the guest soloists. You can find all the albums in the [Discography](../discography/) section.

## 15 years of good music

The Barcelona Big Blues Band was born in 2010 as a bold proposal. And we are still here! Over 500 hand-made arrangements by the band's double bass player and leader Ivan Kovacevic for almost 25 different guests.

The stages of jazz and blues festivals across Spain and the whole of Europe have had the chance to hear this sonic hurricane in full force.

A monthly gig for the last 12 years at the emblematic Barcelona venue Jamboree Jazz Club, which they consider home, has built a solid foundation that lets them keep doing what they do best: making the audience enjoy it the same way they do… With all their soul!
