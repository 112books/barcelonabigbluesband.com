When you use the forms on this website we process the personal data you provide in order to handle your request. Below we explain how.

## Principles we apply

- **Lawfulness, fairness and transparency:** we always ask for your consent and inform you of the purpose in advance.
- **Data minimisation:** we only collect the data strictly necessary.
- **Storage limitation:** we keep the data only for as long as necessary.
- **Integrity and confidentiality:** we apply technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Data you voluntarily provide through the contact and booking forms:

- Name

- Preferred event date (optional)

We do not use this site to collect special categories of data.

## Purpose and legal basis

- **Purpose:** to handle and reply to your enquiries and booking requests.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (article 6.1.a GDPR), given when you submit the form.

We will not use your data for purposes other than those stated without informing you beforehand.

The data is sent to the controller's contact email shown on the contact page and is not shared with third parties, except for the providers strictly necessary to deliver the service (web hosting and email), which process the data as processors under the GDPR. No international data transfers take place.
